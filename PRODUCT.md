# PractoCore

## Register

product

## Users

Ugandan litigators and legal teams managing active matters, court deadlines, research, drafting, and firm document libraries. They work in high-consequence, interruption-heavy workflows and need to find, verify, and act on legal information quickly.

## Product Purpose

PractoCore brings case management, legal research grounded in Ugandan authorities, document drafting, deadline calculation, and shared firm knowledge into one working environment. Success means lawyers can move from source material to a defensible next action without losing context or overlooking an obligation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and dependable. The product should feel like a capable legal workspace: confident without being theatrical, concise without becoming cryptic, and helpful without obscuring the source material.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI chat surfaces that detach answers from evidence, decorative legal-tech styling, dense enterprise dashboards with unclear priority, and novel controls where familiar document or case-management conventions would be faster.

## Design Principles

1. Keep evidence close to every conclusion.
2. Preserve the lawyer's place and context across documents, matters, and conversations.
3. Make consequential actions explicit, reviewable, and reversible where possible.
4. Favor familiar professional-tool patterns so the interface recedes behind the work.
5. Use progressive disclosure to keep complex legal workflows calm and legible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast in light and dark themes, reduced-motion preferences, and clear status communication that does not rely on color alone. Interactions should remain usable on desktop and touch devices, with desktop optimized for sustained document work.
